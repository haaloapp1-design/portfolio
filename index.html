About me

I am a software engineer from Nawabshah, Sindh, who spends the school day teaching and the evenings building.

I graduated in Software Engineering from Riphah International University, Islamabad, and have been teaching since. The two jobs feed each other. A form my colleagues fill in every week became a Chrome extension. A city with no ride service became Haalo. Children near me who are not in school became Parhai Dost.

I like small, useful software that runs well on ordinary phones and PCs, costs little to run, and can be explained to a class of fifteen-year-olds.
